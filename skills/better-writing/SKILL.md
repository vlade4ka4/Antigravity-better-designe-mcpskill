# Better Writing

Produces clear, verb-driven, actionable, and empathetic product copy.

## Rules
- **Action Buttons**: Start with a verb (`Delete project`, never `OK` or `Yes`).
- **Destination Links**: Name destination clearly (`View billing documentation`, never `Click here`).
- **Tone**: Default to sentence case. Positive phrasing for ON toggles (`Send email notifications`).
- **Errors**: Explain what happened and how to resolve it next to the affected control, without blame, "Oops", or exclamation marks.
- **Confirmations & Empty States**: See `./patterns.md` for confirmation dialogs and empty state templates.
